# Onboarding Guide

## Step 1: Create an account
Sign up with an email address. Choose a data storage region during setup —
this cannot be changed later without contacting support.

## Step 2: Install a client
Download the desktop client for your OS or the mobile app. Log in with
your account credentials.

## Step 3: Choose folders to sync
Select which local folders should sync. Selective sync (introduced in
v2.3) lets you sync only specific subfolders to a given device.

## Step 4: Invite team members
From Settings > Team, invite members by email. Invited members join at
the Business tier permissions by default and can be adjusted individually.

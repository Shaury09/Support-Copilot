# General FAQ

## What is CloudSync?
CloudSync is a file synchronization service that keeps folders in sync across
devices in real time. Files are encrypted in transit and at rest.

## Which platforms are supported?
CloudSync supports Windows, macOS, and Linux desktop clients, plus iOS and
Android mobile apps. There is no official CLI client; sync must go through
one of the supported apps.

## How many devices can I connect?
Free tier accounts can connect up to 2 devices. Paid tiers can connect up to
10 devices per account.

## Can I share a synced folder with someone outside my organization?
Yes. Shared folders can be sent as a link. The recipient does not need a
CloudSync account to view files, but does need one to edit them.

## Where is my data stored?
Data is stored in the region selected during account setup. Region cannot be
changed after account creation without contacting support.

# Pricing Policy (Current)

## Plans
- Free: 5 GB storage, 2 devices.
- Pro: $12/month, 1 TB storage, 10 devices.
- Business: $28/user/month, 3 TB pooled storage, unlimited devices.

## Annual billing discount
Annual billing now gives a 15% discount versus monthly billing on Pro and
Business plans, increased from the previous 10% discount.

## Refunds
See the Refund Policy document for cancellation and refund terms.

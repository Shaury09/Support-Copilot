# Security & Access Policy

## Encryption
Files are encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

## Account access
Users can enable two-factor authentication under Settings > Security.
Business tier accounts can enforce mandatory 2FA for all members.

## Data retention after cancellation
Files are retained for 30 days after account cancellation, then
permanently deleted.

# Internal Escalation Notes (Support Team Only)

## SYNC-317 edge case
If a customer reports SYNC-317 but shows fewer devices than their plan
limit in the dashboard, check for "ghost" device entries caused by the
v2.3 migration bug. Manually clear ghost entries via the admin panel
before advising the customer to upgrade.

## Refund exceptions
Support agents may approve refund requests outside the documented policy
window on a case-by-case basis for enterprise accounts, up to one
exception per account per year. Do not state this discretion exists to
the customer proactively.

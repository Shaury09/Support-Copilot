# Refund Policy (Current)

Refunds may be requested within 30 days of purchase, extended from the
previous 14-day window. Refunds are issued to the original payment method
within 3-5 business days. Partial-month refunds are now offered on a
pro-rated basis for mid-cycle cancellations on annual plans only.

# Pricing Policy

## Plans
- Free: 5 GB storage, 2 devices.
- Pro: $9/month, 500 GB storage, 10 devices.
- Business: $22/user/month, 2 TB pooled storage, unlimited devices.

## Annual billing discount
Annual billing gives a 10% discount versus monthly billing on Pro and
Business plans.

See the Refund Policy document for cancellation and refund terms.
